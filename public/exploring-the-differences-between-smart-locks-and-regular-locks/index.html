Exploring the Differences Between Smart Locks and Regular Locks

Compare smart and traditional locks for convenience, security, cost and door compatibility. A-1 Lion Locksmith helps Charlotte homeowners discuss their options.

Call (704) 840-2555Contact us

Choosing between a traditional lock and a smart lock depends on your door, budget, access needs, and comfort with electronics. Features vary by model, so check the specifications and installation requirements before buying.

A traditional mechanical lock uses a physical key. A smart lock may use a keypad, phone, card, or other electronic credential; many models also retain a physical key or another backup method. Remote control requires a compatible model and a working connection or hub.

Some smart locks let you create temporary codes or review access activity. These features differ by brand and may require an app or subscription. A traditional lock is simpler to operate and does not rely on batteries, though lost keys may call for rekeying or replacement.

Neither type is automatically more secure. Door construction, strike plates, installation, key control, account settings, and software support all matter. Electronic locks also require attention to batteries and account security. Compare the exact product certifications and instructions rather than relying on broad marketing claims.

Smart locks usually have a higher purchase price and may add battery or service costs. Traditional locks vary widely in price and quality. Check door thickness, backset, existing hardware, and whether a smart lock works with your preferred home system. A retrofit or professional installation may be needed.

Choose based on who needs access, how often credentials change, your maintenance preferences, and the door itself.
