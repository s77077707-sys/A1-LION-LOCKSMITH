Car lockouts and key options

Car key systems differ by year and trim. A standard metal key, transponder, proximity fob, or push-to-start setup can require different equipment and parts. Describe the dashboard message or ignition problem if the key turns but the vehicle will not start.

We cannot promise a replacement or programming for every vehicle before checking compatibility and availability. Call with the VIN if you have it handy, but do not delay a lockout call to locate it.

Common questions

Automotive Locksmith in Charlotte, NC: FAQ

Can you unlock a car without a spare key?

Tell us the make, model, year, and location. We can discuss lockout options for your vehicle and verify authorization before opening it.

Can you make a new car key if mine is lost?

Replacement depends on the vehicle and the key system. Call with the vehicle details so we can check parts, equipment, and availability.

Do you program key fobs?

Programming is available only when we can support the specific vehicle and fob. We will check compatibility before promising the service.

What if my key is stuck or broken?

Avoid forcing the key or ignition. Explain whether it is stuck in a door, trunk, or ignition and whether any part is visible.
