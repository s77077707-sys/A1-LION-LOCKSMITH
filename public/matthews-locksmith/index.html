Local service guidance

What to know before calling

A move is a good time to decide who should still have access. Rekeying can change the key for compatible, working locks without replacing every piece of hardware. If the lock is worn, the door is misaligned, or you want a different deadbolt, installation or repair may be the better conversation. We can help compare those options after you describe the door.

If you are already locked out, call directly and say whether a key is inside, missing, or broken. Have proof that you are authorized to enter the property. We will confirm availability, discuss the likely approach, and give an estimate before work begins.

A compatible lock in good condition may be rekeyed instead. We can discuss the hardware and the access concern.
