Local service guidance

What to know before calling

A useful first distinction is whether you still have a working key. If a key is locked inside a vehicle, give us the year, make, and model. If you have moved into a home and want previous keys to stop working, count the doors and describe the locks. Those details make the initial conversation more useful than simply saying you need a locksmith.

For a broken or stuck key, avoid pushing the piece deeper into the lock. Let us know which door or vehicle is affected and whether another entrance or spare key is available. We will discuss possible next steps and an estimate after hearing the details.

Availability and arrival time depend on the exact address and time of day. Call (704) 840-2555 to confirm.
