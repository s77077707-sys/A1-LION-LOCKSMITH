Local service guidance

What to know before calling

For a vehicle issue, the year, make and model matter because key and fob systems differ. A simple lockout is a different request from a lost push-to-start fob, and the available work must be checked for that vehicle. For a house, let us know if the concern is an immediate lockout or changing which keys operate several doors.

Homeowners considering a deadbolt or smart lock can describe the existing door and product they have in mind. If a spare key or another entry point exists, mention it before we plan the visit. We will discuss timing and pricing once we understand the specific request.

Availability and arrival time depend on the exact address and time of day. Call (704) 840-2555 to confirm.
