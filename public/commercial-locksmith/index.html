At A-1 Lion Locksmith, we provide a comprehensive range of commercial locksmith services to protect and secure your business. Our skilled technicians are well-equipped to handle all your security needs, ensuring a safe and efficient work environment. Our services include:

Commercial Lock Installation: We specialize in installing high-security locks for businesses, including deadbolts, electronic locks, and access control systems, to enhance security and control access to your premises.

Master Key System Installation: Simplify key management and improve security with a master key system tailored to your business needs. Our locksmiths can design and install a master key system that provides customized access levels for different employees or departments.

Keyless Entry System Installation: Upgrade your business’s security and convenience with a keyless entry system. We can install and program electronic locks, keypad entry systems, and smart locks to enhance access control and streamline entry for employees and authorized personnel.

Access Control System Installation and Maintenance: Protect sensitive areas of your business with an access control system. Our technicians can install, configure, and maintain access control systems that use keycards, biometric authentication, or PIN codes to restrict access to authorized personnel only.

Commercial Lock Rekeying and Key Duplication: Maintain the security of your business premises by rekeying locks when keys are lost or stolen. We also offer key duplication services to ensure that your employees have access to the areas they need.

Lock Repair and Maintenance: Keep your business’s locks in top condition with our lock repair and maintenance services. Whether your locks are damaged or simply need adjustment, our technicians can provide prompt and professional repairs to ensure the security of your premises.

Panic Bar Installation and Repair: Ensure the safety of your employees and customers with panic bars installed on emergency exit doors. We can install, repair, or replace panic bars to ensure compliance with safety regulations and provide quick and easy exit in case of an emergency.

Commercial Safe Installation and Opening: Protect your valuable assets with a commercial safe installed by our experts. We can also provide safe opening services in case of a lockout or forgotten combination.

Security Assessment and Consultation: Worried about the security of your business? Our locksmiths can conduct a comprehensive security assessment and provide personalized recommendations to address vulnerabilities and enhance security measures.

Emergency Locksmith Services: Call to discuss urgent lockouts, damaged locks, or other business lock concerns and confirm availability.
