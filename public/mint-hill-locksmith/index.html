Local service guidance

What to know before calling

If a door will not open, describe whether the key is inside, broken, or turning without releasing the lock. For rekeying, tell us how many doors you want changed and whether any working keys remain. A useful description of the existing hardware helps us decide what to discuss before a visit.

If you are considering replacing a deadbolt instead, describe the door and the lock you have now. We will talk through repair, rekeying, or replacement options as appropriate, and explain pricing before work. Please have proof that you are authorized to access the home.

Availability and arrival time depend on the exact address and time of day. Call (704) 840-2555 to confirm.

Common questions

Can you help with a home lockout in Mint Hill?

Call with your address and describe the door and key problem. We will confirm availability and discuss the next step.

Can I rekey without changing the whole lock?

Often, if the current lock is compatible and in usable condition. Describe the hardware so we can assess the option.

What if a key is broken in the door?

Avoid forcing the key. Tell us which lock is affected and whether any part of the key is visible.
