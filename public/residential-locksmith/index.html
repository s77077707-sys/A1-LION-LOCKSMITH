Rekeying, deadbolts and smart locks

Rekeying changes which key operates a compatible lock while keeping usable hardware. A damaged lock or a door without suitable preparation may call for repair or replacement instead. Tell us if several doors should share one key.

Common questions

Residential Locksmith in Charlotte, NC: FAQ

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

Rekeying may work when the existing hardware is compatible and in good condition. A damaged or unsuitable lock may need repair or replacement.

Can one key work on several doors?

That may be possible when the locks are compatible. Tell us the brands and number of doors so we can review the options.

Can you install a smart lock I purchased?

Call with the product model and door details. We can assess whether the lock is suitable for the existing door and hardware.

Do I need proof that I can authorize the work?

Yes. Have identification and proof that you own, rent, or are authorized to manage the property ready before service begins.
